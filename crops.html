Know Your Crop Before You Grow

Explore key crop requirements, growth priorities and useful management highlights. Actual practices should always be adapted to local soil, weather and agricultural guidance.

QUICK LEARNING GUIDE

Five Important Crop Groups

Each card highlights the essentials at a glance and gives more context than a simple crop description.

🌾 STAPLE CEREAL

Rice

Rice is an important cereal crop widely associated with warm conditions and careful water management. Variety selection and crop calendar should match the local season.

🌡️ Best FocusWarm growing conditions
💧 WaterRegular, well-managed supply
🌱 Field PriorityHealthy establishment and monitoring

Key management idea

Pay close attention to field preparation, crop stage, water needs and early detection of problems. Avoid a one-size-fits-all schedule.

🌾 COOL-SEASON CEREAL

Wheat

Wheat generally benefits from suitable soil conditions, a well-planned growing window and balanced management through its growth stages.

🗓️ TimingMatch sowing to local season
🌱 SoilGood structure and drainage
🔎 MonitorGrowth, moisture and nutrition

Key management idea

Good crop establishment and timely observation are central to managing a wheat field effectively.

🧵 COMMERCIAL FIBRE

Cotton

Cotton is a commercial fibre crop that benefits from thoughtful soil preparation, balanced crop care and regular field scouting.

☀️ ClimateWarm growing environment
🌿 SoilGood drainage is important
🔍 ScoutingRegular crop observation

Key management idea

Track crop development consistently and seek local expert advice when nutrient, pest or disease concerns appear.

🌽 VERSATILE CEREAL

Maize

Maize is used for food, feed and several industrial purposes. Uniform establishment and appropriate spacing can support even crop growth.

🌱 EstablishUniform crop stand
💧 MoistureManage key growth stages
📈 UseFood, feed and industry

Key management idea

Watch early growth and later critical stages, while adapting irrigation and nutrient decisions to actual field conditions.

🥬 HIGH-VALUE DIVERSITY

Vegetables

Vegetable farming includes many crops with different maturity periods, market requirements and water needs, making planning especially important.

🥕 DiversityMany crop and variety choices
💧 PrecisionCareful water management
🧺 QualityHarvest and handling matter

Key management idea

Plan for crop calendar, field hygiene, regular monitoring and post-harvest handling to protect quality.
